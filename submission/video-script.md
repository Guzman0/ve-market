# Pitch video script (2 minutes)

Record your screen with your voice over it. Before recording: Seller account has a veMEZO or veBTC lock, Buyer account has about 100 MUSD, and the live app is open.

| Time | On screen | Say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00 to 0:15 | Deck cover | "Mezo locks can't be opened early. A veMEZO lock can run for four years. If you need your money before then, you wait. Unlocked fixes that." |
| 0:15 to 0:30 | Deck problem slide | "Mezo's own roadmap names this: users who want to exit a lock early. Unlocked is a market where you sell the whole lock for MUSD." |
| 0:30 to 0:55 | App, Seller account, Sell tab | "I'm the seller. Here are my locks, read straight from Mezo. I pick one, choose a discount, and the app shows what I receive and what the buyer earns. Two confirmations and it's listed." (List the lock.) |
| 0:55 to 1:25 | App, switch to Buyer account, Market tab | "Now I'm a buyer. Every listing shows what the lock is worth today, the discount, time to unlock and the yearly return if I hold it. I sign once and confirm once." (Buy it.) "The lock is in my wallet and the seller has their MUSD, in one transaction." |
| 1:25 to 1:40 | Explorer page for the purchase | "Here's the purchase on Mezo testnet." |
| 1:40 to 1:52 | Deck safety or alternatives slide | "Listed locks are held by the contract so they can't change under a buyer, offers check the lock before they settle, and it's tested against Mezo's live contracts." |
| 1:52 to 2:00 | Deck close | "Unlocked: your lock, sold today. Built on Mezo, paid in MUSD." |

Tips: keep each wallet pop-up on screen for a second so judges see it; trim waiting time for confirmations in editing.
